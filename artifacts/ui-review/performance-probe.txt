Method: The probe includes the production src/save_queue.rs directly, substitutes a 500 ms sleeping writer and counts its calls. It creates a GTK TextBuffer holding 10 KiB and 100 synthetic notes initially holding 1 KiB each, then performs 100 inserts at 2 ms intervals. A callback reproduces text extraction, note lookup/update, revision update, and one GTK status-label update. One write is already in flight before the burst; a 16 ms main-loop timer polls and sends the latest snapshot after completion. A 5 ms GLib timer records its maximum observed interval. Assertions check the 8 ms p95 and 50 ms heartbeat targets and two writes.

Limits: This is a synthetic callback, not instrumentation inside the running application. It does not include the complete production mark function, card refresh, visible window rendering, window movement, input-to-frame latency, actual disk I/O, real hardware, or Wayland. It establishes that this test's main loop keeps running while the included queue's synthetic writer sleeps; it does not establish that repaint or window movement remains responsive in the full app. Timing varies with load and environment. The artificial writer is not a persistence or durability test.
